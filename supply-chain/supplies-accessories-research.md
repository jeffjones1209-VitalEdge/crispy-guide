# VItalEdge - Supplies & Accessories Dropshipping Research

## Product Categories to Sell

### 1. Insulin Syringes
- 100-unit (1ml) U-100 insulin syringes — most common for peptide research
- 50-unit (0.5ml) — for smaller doses
- 30G and 31G needle tips
- Sold in boxes of 100

### 2. Alcohol Prep Pads / Wipes
- Individually wrapped 70% isopropyl alcohol wipes
- Bulk boxes (100, 200, 500 count)
- Sterile, medical-grade

### 3. Bacteriostatic Water
- Multi-use vials (30ml)
- 0.9% benzyl alcohol preserved
- Essential companion product to peptides

### 4. Reusable Peptide Pens
- Auto-injector pens for research use
- Compatible with standard peptide vials
- Adjustable dosing

### 5. Additional Accessories
- Sharps disposal containers
- Vial storage cases
- Mixing vials and beakers
- Gloves (nitrile, powder-free)
