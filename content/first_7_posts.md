> 
> Here is how to maintain absolute stability: 👇
> 
> **Post 2/4:**
> 🧊 **Lyophilized (Dry Powder) Storage:**
> Before reconstitution, peptide vials are vacuum-sealed freeze-dried powders. 
> * **Short-term (1-3 months):** Refrigerate at 2°C to 8°C.
> * **Long-term (Up to 2 years):** Store in a deep freezer at -20°C. Keep away from light.
> 
> **Post 3/4:**
> 🧪 **Reconstituted (Liquid) Storage:**
> Once you add Bacteriostatic Water, the peptide bond degradation countdown begins.
> * **Strict Rule:** Store liquid peptides in the refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C.
