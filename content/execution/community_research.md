# VItalEdge Online Community Research & Sourcing Strategy

To drive highly targeted, organic, and high-intent traffic to the VItalEdge web application, we must seed our educational assets inside platforms where the peptide and biohacking communities are most active.

This document identifies **5 high-value digital communities**, outlines their rules, analyzes their pain points, and establishes a tailored organic content distribution strategy for each.

---

## 🌐 1. Reddit: `r/peptides` (60k+ Members)
The absolute epicenter of online peptide discussion, research sharing, and community support.

* **Audience Vibe:** Highly technical, research-oriented, and active. Members consist of self-experimenters, biological researchers, and longevity advocates.
* **Core Community Pain Points:** 
  * Extreme confusion surrounding reconstitution math for high-mg vials (e.g., GHK-Cu 50mg, Tirzepatide 15mg).
  * Misinterpreting U-100 syringe tick marks.
  * Sourcing quality issues and verifying HPLC/MS purity testing documents.
* **Strict Rules to Note:** 
  * **Strict Sourcing Ban:** Do NOT promote supplier links, vendor discounts, or peptide sales. Doing so results in an immediate permanent ban.
  * **Research-Only Framing:** All posts must discuss compounds in a "laboratory research" or "chemical analysis" context.
* **VItalEdge Sourcing Strategy:**
  * **The Content Piece:** Post the **"Master Peptide Reconstitution Cheatsheet"** as a comprehensive, formatting-optimized text guide.
  * **The Traffic Funnel (No-Sourcing Compliant):** Frame the post as a 100% free community utility: *"I got tired of doing manual reconstitution math on my phone, so I built a 100% free visual syringe simulator with no signup or paywall. Let me know if this helps your lab!"* 
  * **Expected Outcome:** Massive organic upvotes, bookmarks/saves, and direct click-throughs to our free calculator on a highly aligned domain.

## 🧬 2. Reddit: `r/biohacking` (200k+ Members)
A massive, rapidly growing community focused on lifestyle optimization, longevity, cellular therapy, and supplements.

* **Audience Vibe:** Highly analytical, open-minded, but skeptical of marketing hype. Members value objective data, scientific journal citations, and peer-reviewed safety profiles.
* **Core Community Pain Points:**
  * Comparing compound synergies (e.g., "Should I run BPC-157 and TB-500 together?").
  * Mitigating age-related cellular decline (high interest in GHK-Cu, Epitalon, NAD+).
  * Standard storage guidelines (avoiding product degradation).
* **Strict Rules to Note:**
  * No commercial self-promotion without heavy value-first educational content.
  * No medical advice claims (posts must be informational).
* **VItalEdge Sourcing Strategy:**
  * **The Content Piece:** Post a detailed comparison article: **"BPC-157 vs. TB-500: The Biocellular Chemistry of Connective Tissue Repair."** Summarize the cellular receptors involved (growth hormone fibroblast upregulation vs. actin sequestration) with citations.
  * **The Traffic Funnel:** Embed the link at the end of the science-dive: *"If your research lab is testing both compounds, you can automate your co-administration reconstitution mathematics using this free visual tool: [Link]"*.
