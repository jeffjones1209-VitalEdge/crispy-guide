## 📹 2. YouTube Brand Account Setup
A YouTube Brand Account allows multiple team members (or social media managers) to manage the channel without sharing personal Google Account passwords.

### Step-by-Step Channel Creation:
1. **Google Account:** Sign in to the primary corporate Google Account.
2. **Channel Creation:** Go to the [YouTube Channel Switcher](https://www.youtube.com/channel_switcher) and click **Create a Channel**.
3. **Brand Name:** Enter `VItalEdge` as the brand channel name and accept terms.
4. **Account Verification (Crucial):** Go to **YouTube Studio** > **Settings** > **Channel** > **Feature Eligibility**.
   * Verify via SMS to unlock **Intermediate Features** (allows custom thumbnails, and vertical/short-form uploads longer than 60 seconds if needed).
   * Submit ID or Video Verification to unlock **Advanced Features** (enables clickable external links in YouTube Shorts descriptions).
5. **Customization & Links:** In YouTube Studio, click **Customization** > **Basic Info**.
   * Add the brand handle `@VItalEdgeDose`.
   * Add the link to the free web application: Title: `Free Reconstitution Calculator`, URL: `[Your Production URL]`.
6. **Channel Layout:** Upload a minimalist, dark-teal high-tech square logo (avatar) and a custom 2560x1440 header banner depicting peptide structures and the slogan *"Precision in Research, Trust in Science."*

## 📸 3. Instagram Creator/Professional Account Setup
An Instagram Professional Account provides deep analytics, allows post-scheduling, and integrates with comment automation software like ManyChat.

### Step-by-Step Account Creation:
1. **Sign Up:** Register using the official corporate email (`social@vitaledge.com`) and choose the handle `@VItalEdgeDose`.
2. **Switch to Professional Account:** Go to **Settings** > **Account Type and Tools** > **Switch to Professional Account**.
3. **Category Selection:** Select **Education**, **Health/Beauty**, or **Science/Technology** as the category. Choose **Creator** (ideal for video-focused content with access to trending audio library).
4. **Bio Optimization:** Use standard bulleted layout:

## 🎵 4. TikTok Creator/Business Account Setup
TikTok's algorithm provides the highest rate of organic top-of-funnel reach for vertical video content, making it vital for early-stage user acquisition.

### Step-by-Step Account Creation:
1. **Sign Up:** Register on the mobile app or web using the corporate email and secure password. Handle: `@VItalEdgeDose`.
2. **Account Optimization:** Enter the display name `VItalEdge`.
3. **The Link-in-Bio Constraint (Important):** 
   * TikTok limits active external hyperlinks in the profile to accounts with **1,000+ followers** (for Creator profiles) or verified **Business Accounts** (requires uploading corporate registration documents).
   * **Staged Strategy:** 
     * *Stage 1 (Under 1000 Followers):* Keep the account as a **Creator Account** to access the full Commercial & Trending Music Library (crucial for short-form algorithm growth). Format the website URL in plain text in the bio: `VItalEdge.com (Calculators)`.
     * *Stage 2 (Over 1000 Followers):* Switch the creator profile to add the live clickable link in the dedicated website field.
4. **Safety & Comment Filters:** Go to **Settings and Privacy** > **Privacy** > **Comments**.
   * Turn on **Filter keywords**. Add terms like: *buy, sell, discount, source, quality, inject, syringe, human use*. This prevents comments that promote illicit peptide sourcing or violate TikTok's medical/supplement guidelines.
