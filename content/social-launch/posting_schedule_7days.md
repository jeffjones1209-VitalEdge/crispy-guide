## 🚀 3. Platform-Specific Workflow Execution

To maintain sanity while publishing across multiple channels, follow this operational workflow:

### A. The "Short-Form Video" Workflow (Days 1, 5, 6)
1. **Record:** Shoot A-Roll (talking head) and B-Roll (overhead syringe handling) in 4K 60fps vertical format.
2. **Edit:** Add auto-captions via CapCut. Apply bright, bold text (e.g., Montserrat/Futura font). Use transitions at least every 3 seconds to retain attention.
3. **Upload:** Upload natively to TikTok first (utilizing trending commercial audio at 5% volume).
4. **Repurpose:** Export the edited video *without* the TikTok watermark (using tools like SnapTik) and upload natively to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels to protect algorithmic distribution.

### B. The "Instagram Carousel" Workflow (Days 2, 3, 4, 7)
1. **Export Slides:** Export slides created from our Canva templates in high-res PNG format.
2. **Verify Swipe:** Ensure text elements on slide boundaries align perfectly to entice a seamless "swipe-to-read" behavior.
3. **Draft Caption:** Keep the main caption structured with clear bullet points. Leave the mandatory safety disclaimer at the absolute bottom.
4. **Automation Link:** Test the ManyChat trigger link before publishing.
