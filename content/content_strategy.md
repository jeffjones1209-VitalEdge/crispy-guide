## 5. Medical Disclaimer & Compliance Guidelines

To protect the VItalEdge brand, maintain absolute transparency, and comply with platform advertising/content guidelines, we implement an uncompromised legal framing across all channels.

### The Immutable Rules of Peptide Content:
1. **Never use first-person human ingestion terms:** Avoid phrases like "when you inject," "your dosage," "take this," or "side effects you will feel."
2. **Always use scientific, laboratory research terms:** Use "reconstitution of the research compound," "concentration in the vial," "subjects of study," "in-vitro/in-vivo research," or "liquid measurement."
3. **Always frame physical actions as demonstrations:** In reconstitution videos, explicitly state: *"This is a laboratory demonstration showing how to dilute a lyophilized compound using colored saline for measurement purposes."*
4. **Mandatory Disclaimer on All Posts:** Every caption, thread, and video description must carry a visible disclaimer.

### Standard Disclaimer Text:
> **Disclaimer:** *For educational, informational, and laboratory research purposes only. Peptides are not approved for human consumption, medical diagnosis, treatment, or prevention of any disease. All calculations are theoretical. VItalEdge does not provide medical advice or encourage self-administration. Consult a licensed medical professional for health concerns.*
